Serge Haroche

  • Lifespan: 1944 – present
  • Fields: Physics
  • Countries: France

Overview

Serge Haroche developed methods to measure and manipulate individual quantum systems.

Early Life

Born in Casablanca, Morocco. Studied at the Ecole Normale Superieure in Paris.

Impact

His techniques for trapping photons enabled direct observation of quantum decoherence.
