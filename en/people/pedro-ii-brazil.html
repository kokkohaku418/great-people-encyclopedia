Pedro II of Brazil

  • Lifespan: 1825 – 1891
  • Fields: Politics
  • Countries: Brazil

Overview

Pedro II was the second and last Emperor of Brazil who ruled for 58 years, overseeing modernization and the abolition of slavery.

Early Life

Born in Rio de Janeiro. Became emperor at age five after his father's abdication.

Impact

Pedro II modernized Brazil, promoted science and culture, and ultimately abolished slavery, though this contributed to the end of the monarchy.
