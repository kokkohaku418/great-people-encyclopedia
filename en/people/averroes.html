Averroes

  • Lifespan: 1126 – 1198
  • Fields: Philosophy
  • Countries: Spain

Overview

Averroes (Ibn Rushd) was an Andalusian-Arab philosopher and jurist who wrote extensive commentaries on Aristotle, earning him the title 'The Commentator' in medieval Europe.

Early Life

Averroes was born in Córdoba, Al-Andalus. From a family of jurists, he studied law, medicine, and philosophy, becoming chief judge of Córdoba.

Impact

Averroes' commentaries on Aristotle preserved and transmitted Greek philosophy to medieval Europe, catalyzing the Scholastic movement. His defense of philosophy's autonomy influenced Aquinas and European thought.
