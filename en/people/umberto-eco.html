Umberto Eco

  • Lifespan: 1932 – 2016
  • Fields: Philosophy, Literature
  • Countries: Italy

Overview

Umberto Eco was an Italian semiotician, philosopher, and novelist whose works bridged academic semiotics and popular fiction.

Early Life

Born in Alessandria, Piedmont, he studied at the University of Turin and became a leading figure in semiotics before achieving worldwide fame as a novelist.

Impact

He demonstrated that intellectually rigorous fiction could reach a mass audience, and his semiotic theories influenced communication studies, aesthetics, and cultural criticism.
