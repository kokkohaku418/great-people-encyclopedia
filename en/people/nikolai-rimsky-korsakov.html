Nikolai Rimsky-Korsakov

  • Lifespan: 1844 – 1908
  • Fields: Art
  • Countries: Russia

Overview

Nikolai Rimsky-Korsakov was a Russian composer known for his orchestral brilliance and his operas based on Russian folklore.

Early Life

Born in Tikhvin, Russia. Trained as a naval officer while studying music.

Impact

Rimsky-Korsakov's orchestration treatise became the standard reference, and his operas and orchestral works showcased Russian musical traditions.
