Rudolf Diesel

  • Lifespan: 1858 – 1913
  • Fields: Engineering
  • Countries: Germany

Overview

Rudolf Diesel was a German engineer who invented the diesel engine, a more efficient alternative to the steam and gasoline engines of his era.

Early Life

Born in Paris to Bavarian immigrants, he studied engineering at the Royal Bavarian Polytechnic in Munich and became obsessed with creating a more efficient engine.

Impact

The diesel engine he invented became the dominant power source for heavy transport, shipping, and industry, and his vision of running engines on plant oils anticipated modern biofuels.
