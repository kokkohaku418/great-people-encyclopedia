Jean-Paul Sartre

  • Lifespan: 1905 – 1980
  • Fields: Philosophy, Literature
  • Countries: France

Overview

Jean-Paul Sartre was a French existentialist philosopher, playwright, and novelist. He was one of the leading intellectual figures of the 20th century.

Early Life

Sartre was born in Paris. He studied at the École Normale Supérieure and taught philosophy before becoming a full-time writer after World War II.

Impact

Sartre's existentialism, centered on radical freedom and responsibility, profoundly influenced philosophy, literature, and political activism. He declined the Nobel Prize in Literature in 1964.
