Toyotomi Hideyori

  • Lifespan: 1593 – 1615
  • Fields: Politics
  • Countries: Japan

Overview

Toyotomi Hideyori was the son and heir of Toyotomi Hideyoshi who fought against the Tokugawa in the Siege of Osaka.

Early Life

Born at Osaka Castle as son of the great unifier.

Impact

His defeat at Osaka marked the final consolidation of Tokugawa power.
