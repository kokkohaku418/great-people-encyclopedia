Louise Erdrich

  • Lifespan: 1954 – present
  • Fields: Literature
  • Countries: United States

Overview

Louise Erdrich is an Ojibwe author whose novels chronicle Native American life in the northern Midwest.

Early Life

Born in Little Falls, Minnesota. Grew up in Wahpeton, North Dakota and studied at Dartmouth and Johns Hopkins.

Impact

Her interconnected novels created a vast literary landscape of Native American experience comparable to Faulkner's Yoknapatawpha.
