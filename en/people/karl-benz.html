Karl Benz

  • Lifespan: 1844 – 1929
  • Fields: Engineering
  • Countries: Germany

Overview

Karl Benz was a German engine designer and automobile engineer who created the first practical automobile powered by an internal combustion engine.

Early Life

Born in Muhlburg, Baden, he studied mechanical engineering at the University of Karlsruhe and dedicated himself to creating a motorized vehicle.

Impact

His Patent-Motorwagen of 1886 is widely regarded as the first true automobile, and his wife Bertha's famous long-distance drive proved its practicality to the world.
