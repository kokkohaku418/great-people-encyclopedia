Aung San

  • Lifespan: 1915 – 1947
  • Fields: Politics
  • Countries: mm

Overview

Aung San was the founder of modern Burma (Myanmar) and the chief architect of Burmese independence.

Early Life

Born in Natmauk, central Burma. Studied at Rangoon University where he became a student leader.

Impact

He negotiated Burma's independence from Britain and is revered as the father of the nation.
