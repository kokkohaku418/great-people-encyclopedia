Kim Il-sung

  • Lifespan: 1912 – 1994
  • Fields: Politics
  • Countries: kp

Overview

Kim Il-sung was the founder and first leader of North Korea, ruling for nearly five decades.

Early Life

Born in Mangyongdae near Pyongyang. Fought as a guerrilla against Japanese occupation in Manchuria.

Impact

He established the North Korean state and the Juche ideology, creating one of the most isolated nations on earth.
