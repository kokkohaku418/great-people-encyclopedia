Fyodor Dostoevsky

  • Lifespan: 1821 – 1881
  • Fields: Literature, Philosophy
  • Countries: Russia

Overview

Fyodor Dostoevsky was a Russian novelist and philosopher whose psychological depth and exploration of the human condition make him one of the greatest novelists in world literature.

Early Life

Dostoevsky was born in Moscow. He was arrested for political activities, sentenced to death, reprieved at the last moment, and sent to a Siberian labor camp for four years.

Impact

Dostoevsky's exploration of free will, suffering, morality, and the human psyche profoundly influenced existentialism, psychology, and modernist literature.
