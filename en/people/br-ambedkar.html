B.R. Ambedkar

  • Lifespan: 1891 – 1956
  • Fields: Politics, Philosophy
  • Countries: India

Overview

B.R. Ambedkar was the chief architect of the Indian Constitution and a champion of the rights of Dalits.

Early Life

Born into a Dalit family in Mhow. Overcame severe discrimination to earn doctorates from Columbia and the London School of Economics.

Impact

He transformed Indian society by fighting caste discrimination and drafting the world's longest written constitution.
