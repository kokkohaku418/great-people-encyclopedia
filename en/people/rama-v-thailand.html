King Chulalongkorn (Rama V)

  • Lifespan: 1853 – 1910
  • Fields: Politics
  • Countries: Thailand

Overview

King Chulalongkorn modernized Siam and preserved its independence during the era of European colonialism.

Early Life

Born in Bangkok as the eldest son of King Mongkut. Educated by both Thai and Western tutors.

Impact

He abolished slavery, reformed the government, and kept Thailand as the only Southeast Asian country never colonized.
