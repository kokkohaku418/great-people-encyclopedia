Li Bai

  • Lifespan: 701 – 762
  • Fields: Literature
  • Countries: China

Overview

Li Bai was a Chinese poet of the Tang dynasty, regarded as one of the two greatest poets in Chinese literary history alongside Du Fu. His romantic, imaginative poetry celebrates nature, friendship, and wine.

Early Life

Li Bai was born in Central Asia and grew up in Sichuan. He traveled widely, gained fame as a poet, and briefly served at the court of Emperor Xuanzong.

Impact

Li Bai's poetry, combining spontaneity with classical mastery, represents the pinnacle of Tang dynasty verse. His works remain among the most beloved in the Chinese literary canon.
