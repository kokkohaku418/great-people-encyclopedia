Georg Simmel

  • Lifespan: 1858 – 1918
  • Fields: Philosophy
  • Countries: Germany

Overview

Georg Simmel was a German sociologist and philosopher who pioneered the study of social interaction, urban life, and the philosophy of money.

Early Life

Born in Berlin to a Jewish family that had converted to Christianity, he studied philosophy and history at the University of Berlin.

Impact

His microsociological approach to studying social forms and interactions influenced the Chicago School of sociology and modern urban studies.
