Noam Chomsky

  • Lifespan: 1928 – present
  • Fields: Philosophy
  • Countries: United States

Overview

Noam Chomsky is an American linguist, philosopher, and political activist who revolutionized linguistics with his theory of generative grammar. He is also one of the most prominent public intellectuals.

Early Life

Chomsky was born in Philadelphia to a family of Jewish immigrants. He studied linguistics and philosophy at the University of Pennsylvania and has spent most of his career at MIT.

Impact

Chomsky's theory of universal grammar transformed linguistics and cognitive science. His political writings on media, foreign policy, and power have made him one of the most cited scholars alive.
