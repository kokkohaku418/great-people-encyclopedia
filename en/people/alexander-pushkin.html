Alexander Pushkin

  • Lifespan: 1799 – 1837
  • Fields: Literature
  • Countries: Russia

Overview

Alexander Pushkin was a Russian poet and novelist who is considered the founder of modern Russian literature. His verse novel Eugene Onegin is a masterpiece of world literature.

Early Life

Pushkin was born in Moscow to an aristocratic family. He was educated at the Imperial Lyceum and gained early fame for his poetry, though his liberal views led to periods of exile.

Impact

Pushkin created the modern Russian literary language and established the foundation upon which Tolstoy, Dostoevsky, and all subsequent Russian literature was built.
