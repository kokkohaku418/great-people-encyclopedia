Miyamoto Musashi

  • Lifespan: 1584 – 1645
  • Fields: Philosophy
  • Countries: Japan

Overview

Miyamoto Musashi was Japan's most legendary swordsman and the author of The Book of Five Rings, a classic treatise on strategy and martial arts.

Early Life

Born in Harima Province, he fought his first duel at age thirteen and went on to be undefeated in over sixty duels throughout his life.

Impact

His philosophical writings on strategy transcended martial arts to influence business, sports, and leadership worldwide.
