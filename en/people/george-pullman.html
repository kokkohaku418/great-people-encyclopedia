George Pullman

  • Lifespan: 1831 – 1897
  • Fields: Engineering
  • Countries: United States

Overview

George Pullman was an American industrialist who revolutionized rail travel by developing the luxury sleeping car and founding the Pullman Palace Car Company.

Early Life

Born in Brocton, New York, he worked as a cabinetmaker before moving to Chicago where he began converting rail cars into sleepers.

Impact

His Pullman sleeping cars transformed long-distance rail travel and his company town experiment, though ultimately controversial, influenced urban planning discussions.
