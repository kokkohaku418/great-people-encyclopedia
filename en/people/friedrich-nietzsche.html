Friedrich Nietzsche

  • Lifespan: 1844 – 1900
  • Fields: Philosophy
  • Countries: Germany

Overview

Friedrich Nietzsche was a German philosopher whose radical ideas on morality, religion, and the will to power profoundly influenced 20th-century thought.

Early Life

Nietzsche was born in Röcken, Prussia. He became professor of classical philology at the University of Basel at the remarkably young age of 24.

Impact

Nietzsche's critique of traditional morality, his concept of the Übermensch, and his proclamation of the death of God transformed philosophy, literature, and psychology.
