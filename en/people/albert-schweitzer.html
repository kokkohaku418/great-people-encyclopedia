Albert Schweitzer

  • Lifespan: 1875 – 1965
  • Fields: Biology, Philosophy
  • Countries: France, Germany

Overview

Albert Schweitzer was an Alsatian-German theologian, philosopher, musician, and physician who devoted his life to medical missionary work in Africa.

Early Life

Born in Kaysersberg, Alsace, he was a renowned organist and Bach scholar who shocked his peers by deciding at age thirty to study medicine and serve in Africa.

Impact

He established a hospital in Lambarene, Gabon, developed the ethic of 'Reverence for Life,' and won the Nobel Peace Prize for his humanitarian work.
