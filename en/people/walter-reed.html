Walter Reed

  • Lifespan: 1851 – 1902
  • Fields: Biology
  • Countries: United States

Overview

Walter Reed was an American army physician who proved that yellow fever is transmitted by mosquitoes, enabling the control of one of the most devastating tropical diseases.

Early Life

Born in Belroi, Virginia, he earned his medical degree at age seventeen and served as a military physician across the American frontier.

Impact

His proof of mosquito transmission of yellow fever enabled the construction of the Panama Canal and saved millions of lives in tropical regions.
