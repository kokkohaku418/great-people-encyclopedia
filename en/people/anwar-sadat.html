Anwar Sadat

  • Lifespan: 1918 – 1981
  • Fields: Politics
  • Countries: Egypt

Overview

Anwar Sadat was the President of Egypt who made the historic peace with Israel, winning the Nobel Peace Prize and fundamentally altering Middle Eastern politics.

Early Life

Born in Mit Abu al-Kum, he was a career military officer who participated in the 1952 revolution and served as Nasser's vice president.

Impact

His visit to Jerusalem and the Camp David Accords broke the cycle of Arab-Israeli wars and established the framework for Middle Eastern peace negotiations.
