Nikolay Basov

  • Lifespan: 1922 – 2001
  • Fields: Physics
  • Countries: Russia

Overview

Nikolay Basov independently developed the maser-laser principle and shared the Nobel Prize for quantum electronics.

Early Life

Born in Usman, Russia. Served in World War II before studying at the Moscow Engineering Physics Institute.

Impact

His work on quantum electronics was essential to the development of lasers.
