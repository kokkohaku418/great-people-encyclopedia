Confucius

  • Lifespan: -551 – -479
  • Fields: Philosophy
  • Countries: China

Overview

Confucius was a Chinese philosopher and teacher whose ideas on ethics, family, and governance profoundly shaped East Asian civilization. His teachings form the basis of Confucianism.

Early Life

Confucius was born in the state of Lu in present-day Shandong province. From a modest background, he devoted himself to learning and teaching from an early age.

Impact

Confucius's emphasis on moral cultivation, filial piety, ritual propriety, and benevolent governance shaped Chinese culture and influenced all of East Asia for over two millennia.
