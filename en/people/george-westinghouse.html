George Westinghouse

  • Lifespan: 1846 – 1914
  • Fields: Engineering
  • Countries: United States

Overview

George Westinghouse was an American entrepreneur and engineer who championed alternating current and made railroad travel safer.

Early Life

Born in Central Bridge, New York. Patented air brake at age 22.

Impact

Westinghouse's adoption of AC power, built on Tesla's patents, won the War of Currents and electrified the world.
