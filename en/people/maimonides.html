Maimonides

  • Lifespan: 1138 – 1204
  • Fields: Philosophy
  • Countries: Spain, Egypt

Overview

Maimonides (Moses ben Maimon) was a medieval Jewish philosopher, physician, and rabbi who is considered the greatest Jewish philosopher of the Middle Ages.

Early Life

Maimonides was born in Córdoba, Al-Andalus. His family fled the Almohad persecution and eventually settled in Egypt, where he became the leader of the Jewish community and court physician.

Impact

Maimonides' Guide for the Perplexed reconciled Aristotelian philosophy with Jewish theology, influencing both Jewish and Christian Scholastic thought. His Mishneh Torah systematized Jewish law.
