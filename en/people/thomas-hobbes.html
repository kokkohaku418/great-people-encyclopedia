Thomas Hobbes

  • Lifespan: 1588 – 1679
  • Fields: Philosophy, Politics
  • Countries: United Kingdom

Overview

Thomas Hobbes was an English philosopher best known for his political philosophy, particularly Leviathan, which argues for a strong sovereign authority to prevent the chaos of the state of nature.

Early Life

Hobbes was born in Westport, Wiltshire. He studied at the University of Oxford and spent time in Continental Europe, where he met Galileo and Descartes.

Impact

Hobbes's social contract theory and his materialist philosophy profoundly influenced political science and modern political thought. Leviathan remains a foundational text of political philosophy.
