Haruki Murakami

  • Lifespan: 1949 – present
  • Fields: Literature
  • Countries: Japan

Overview

Haruki Murakami is a Japanese novelist whose surreal, melancholic fiction blends Western and Japanese literary traditions. He is one of the most widely read contemporary authors.

Early Life

Murakami was born in Kyoto and grew up in Kobe. He ran a jazz bar in Tokyo before becoming a full-time writer after his first novel won a literary prize.

Impact

Murakami's blend of realism and surrealism, Western references, and exploration of loneliness has created a distinctive literary voice that resonates globally.
