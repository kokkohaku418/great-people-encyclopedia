Naguib Mahfouz

  • Lifespan: 1911 – 2006
  • Fields: Literature
  • Countries: Egypt

Overview

Naguib Mahfouz was an Egyptian novelist who was the first Arabic-language author to win the Nobel Prize in Literature. His Cairo Trilogy is considered a masterpiece of Arabic literature.

Early Life

Mahfouz was born in Cairo and spent his entire life there. He studied philosophy at Cairo University and worked as a civil servant while writing prolifically.

Impact

Mahfouz brought the novel form to maturity in Arabic literature. His realistic depictions of Egyptian society and his experimentation with form influenced all subsequent Arabic fiction.
