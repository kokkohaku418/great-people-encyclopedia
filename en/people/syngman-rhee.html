Syngman Rhee

  • Lifespan: 1875 – 1965
  • Fields: Politics
  • Countries: South Korea

Overview

Syngman Rhee was the first president of South Korea and a lifelong independence activist.

Early Life

Born in Haeju, Hwanghae Province. Studied at George Washington University, Harvard, and Princeton.

Impact

He led the Korean independence movement in exile and became South Korea's founding president.
