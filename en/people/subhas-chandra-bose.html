Subhas Chandra Bose

  • Lifespan: 1897 – 1945
  • Fields: Politics
  • Countries: India

Overview

Subhas Chandra Bose was an Indian nationalist leader who sought independence through armed struggle.

Early Life

Born in Cuttack, Orissa. Studied at Cambridge and passed the Indian Civil Service exam but resigned.

Impact

His Indian National Army challenged British rule militarily and accelerated Indian independence.
