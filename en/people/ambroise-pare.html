Ambroise Pare

  • Lifespan: 1510 – 1590
  • Fields: Biology
  • Countries: France

Overview

Ambroise Pare was a French barber-surgeon who is considered the father of modern surgery for his revolutionary techniques in wound treatment and surgical practice.

Early Life

Born in Bourg-Hersent, he trained as a barber-surgeon in Paris and gained his surgical experience on the battlefields of the Italian Wars.

Impact

He replaced the brutal practice of cauterizing wounds with boiling oil with gentler ligature techniques and prosthetic design, transforming surgical practice.
