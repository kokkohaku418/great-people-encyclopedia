Tokugawa Yoshimune

  • Lifespan: 1684 – 1751
  • Fields: Politics
  • Countries: Japan

Overview

Tokugawa Yoshimune was the eighth shogun of the Tokugawa dynasty, known as the 'Rice Shogun' for his sweeping economic and administrative reforms.

Early Life

Born the fourth son of the lord of Kii Province, he unexpectedly became shogun after the deaths of his predecessors without heirs.

Impact

His Kyoho Reforms revitalized the shogunate's finances, promoted Dutch learning that opened Japan to Western science, and established fire brigades in Edo.
