Li Shimin (Tang Taizong)

  • Lifespan: 598 – 649
  • Fields: Politics
  • Countries: China

Overview

Li Shimin, known as Emperor Taizong of Tang, is regarded as one of China's greatest emperors who established the golden age of the Tang Dynasty.

Early Life

Born into an aristocratic family in Wugong, Shaanxi, he was instrumental in helping his father Li Yuan overthrow the Sui Dynasty.

Impact

His reign set the standard for good governance in Chinese history, establishing systems of law, education, and meritocratic civil service that endured for centuries.
