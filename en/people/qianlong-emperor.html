Qianlong Emperor

  • Lifespan: 1711 – 1799
  • Fields: Politics
  • Countries: China

Overview

The Qianlong Emperor presided over the zenith of Qing Dynasty prosperity and territorial expansion during one of the longest reigns in Chinese history.

Early Life

Born Hongli, he was the favorite grandson of the Kangxi Emperor and was carefully groomed for rule from childhood.

Impact

He expanded Chinese territory to its greatest extent, patronized a massive literary compilation project, but his later reign saw the beginning of dynastic decline.
