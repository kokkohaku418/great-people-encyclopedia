Desiderius Erasmus

  • Lifespan: 1466 – 1536
  • Fields: Philosophy, Literature
  • Countries: Netherlands

Overview

Desiderius Erasmus was a Dutch Renaissance humanist, Catholic priest, and theologian. He was the most famous scholar of his time and a leading figure of Christian humanism.

Early Life

Erasmus was born in Rotterdam. Orphaned young, he entered a monastery before traveling throughout Europe, studying at Paris and Cambridge, and building a vast network of scholarly correspondents.

Impact

Erasmus's satirical works and new translations of the Bible promoted educational reform and religious tolerance. His humanism profoundly influenced the Reformation and the development of European intellectual culture.
