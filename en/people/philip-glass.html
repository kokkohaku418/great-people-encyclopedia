Philip Glass

  • Lifespan: 1937 – present
  • Fields: Art
  • Countries: United States

Overview

Philip Glass is an American composer who is one of the most influential musicians of the late 20th century, a pioneer of minimalism.

Early Life

Born in Baltimore, Maryland. Studied at Juilliard and in Paris with Nadia Boulanger.

Impact

Glass's repetitive, hypnotic music broke classical conventions and influenced rock, film, and pop music.
