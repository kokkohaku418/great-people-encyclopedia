Seneca

  • Lifespan: -4 – 65
  • Fields: Philosophy, Literature
  • Countries: Italy

Overview

Seneca was a Roman Stoic philosopher, statesman, and dramatist who served as advisor to Emperor Nero. His letters and essays are major works of Stoic ethics.

Early Life

Seneca was born in Corduba, Hispania. He was educated in Rome in rhetoric and philosophy and rose to political prominence as tutor and advisor to the young Emperor Nero.

Impact

Seneca's Letters and essays made Stoic philosophy accessible and practical. His emphasis on virtue, self-discipline, and the brevity of life continues to resonate widely.
