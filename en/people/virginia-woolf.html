Virginia Woolf

  • Lifespan: 1882 – 1941
  • Fields: Literature
  • Countries: United Kingdom

Overview

Virginia Woolf was an English writer and modernist who pioneered the use of stream of consciousness in fiction. She is considered one of the most important authors of the 20th century.

Early Life

Woolf was born in London to a prominent literary family. She was educated at home and became central to the Bloomsbury Group of intellectuals and artists.

Impact

Woolf's innovations in narrative technique, particularly stream of consciousness, transformed the novel. Her feminist essays, especially A Room of One's Own, remain foundational texts.
