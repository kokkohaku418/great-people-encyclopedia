Reza Shah Pahlavi

  • Lifespan: 1878 – 1944
  • Fields: Politics
  • Countries: Iran

Overview

Reza Shah Pahlavi was the founder of the Pahlavi dynasty who transformed Iran from a feudal state into a modern centralized nation.

Early Life

Born Reza Khan in Mazandaran Province, he rose through the military ranks of the Persian Cossack Brigade to become the most powerful man in Iran.

Impact

He modernized Iran's infrastructure, education, and legal system, banned the veil, and established Iran's first university and railroad.
