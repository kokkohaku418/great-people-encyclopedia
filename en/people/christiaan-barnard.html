Christiaan Barnard

  • Lifespan: 1922 – 2001
  • Fields: Biology
  • Countries: South Africa

Overview

Christiaan Barnard was a South African cardiac surgeon who performed the world's first successful human-to-human heart transplant.

Early Life

Born in Beaufort West, Cape Province, he studied medicine at the University of Cape Town and trained in cardiac surgery at the University of Minnesota.

Impact

His pioneering heart transplant opened the era of organ transplantation and transformed cardiac surgery from an experimental field into a life-saving practice.
