Edgar Allan Poe

  • Lifespan: 1809 – 1849
  • Fields: Literature
  • Countries: United States

Overview

Edgar Allan Poe was an American writer and poet who pioneered the detective fiction and horror genres. His dark, atmospheric tales and poems remain among the most popular in English literature.

Early Life

Poe was born in Boston and orphaned young. He was raised by John Allan in Richmond, Virginia, and had a troubled life marked by poverty, alcoholism, and personal loss.

Impact

Poe invented the detective story genre and his tales of horror and mystery established American Gothic literature. His literary criticism and theories influenced French Symbolism and world literature.
