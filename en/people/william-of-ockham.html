William of Ockham

  • Lifespan: 1287 – 1347
  • Fields: Philosophy
  • Countries: United Kingdom

Overview

William of Ockham was a Franciscan friar and philosopher best known for Ockham's Razor.

Early Life

Born in Ockham, Surrey. Studied at the University of Oxford.

Impact

Ockham's Razor - the principle of parsimony - became a fundamental principle of scientific reasoning.
