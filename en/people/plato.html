Plato

  • Lifespan: -428 – -348
  • Fields: Philosophy
  • Countries: Greece

Overview

Plato was an ancient Greek philosopher who founded the Academy in Athens, the first institution of higher learning in the Western world. His philosophical writings have shaped Western thought for millennia.

Early Life

Plato was born into an aristocratic Athenian family. He became a student of Socrates and was profoundly affected by his teacher's execution in 399 BC.

Impact

Plato's theory of Forms, his political philosophy in the Republic, and his dialogues established the framework for Western metaphysics, epistemology, and political thought.
