Francis Bacon

  • Lifespan: 1561 – 1626
  • Fields: Philosophy
  • Countries: United Kingdom

Overview

Francis Bacon was an English philosopher and statesman who developed the empirical method and is considered the father of empiricism and the scientific method.

Early Life

Bacon was born in London to a prominent political family. He studied at Trinity College, Cambridge, and pursued a career in law and politics alongside his philosophical work.

Impact

Bacon's advocacy of inductive reasoning and empirical observation laid the philosophical groundwork for modern science. His vision of organized scientific research anticipated modern research institutions.
