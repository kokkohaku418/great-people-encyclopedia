Murasaki Shikibu

  • Lifespan: 978 – 1014
  • Fields: Literature
  • Countries: Japan

Overview

Murasaki Shikibu was a Japanese novelist, poet, and lady-in-waiting at the Imperial court. She is best known as the author of The Tale of Genji, often considered the world's first novel.

Early Life

Murasaki was born into a minor branch of the Fujiwara clan. She served at the court of Empress Shōshi and is believed to have written her masterwork there.

Impact

The Tale of Genji is widely considered the world's first novel and a masterpiece of Japanese literature. It profoundly influenced Japanese aesthetics, poetry, and narrative art.
