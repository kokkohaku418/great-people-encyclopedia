Giordano Bruno

  • Lifespan: 1548 – 1600
  • Fields: Philosophy, Astronomy
  • Countries: Italy

Overview

Giordano Bruno was an Italian philosopher and cosmological theorist who proposed that the universe is infinite and contains many worlds. He was burned at the stake for heresy.

Early Life

Bruno was born in Nola, near Naples. He entered the Dominican order but fled after developing unorthodox views, wandering through Europe for years.

Impact

Bruno's vision of an infinite universe with innumerable worlds anticipated modern cosmology. His martyrdom for free thought made him a symbol of intellectual freedom.
