Walt Whitman

  • Lifespan: 1819 – 1892
  • Fields: Literature
  • Countries: United States

Overview

Walt Whitman was an American poet whose Leaves of Grass is a landmark in American literature. His free verse and celebration of democracy, nature, and the body revolutionized poetry.

Early Life

Whitman was born in West Hills, New York. He worked as a journalist, teacher, and government clerk, and volunteered as a nurse during the Civil War.

Impact

Whitman's free verse broke from traditional poetic forms and his celebration of the self, democracy, and physical experience opened new possibilities for poetry worldwide.
