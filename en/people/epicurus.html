Epicurus

  • Lifespan: -341 – -270
  • Fields: Philosophy
  • Countries: Greece

Overview

Epicurus was an ancient Greek philosopher who founded Epicureanism, a philosophy advocating the pursuit of modest pleasures, friendship, and freedom from fear as the path to happiness.

Early Life

Epicurus was born on the island of Samos. He began studying philosophy at age 14 and later founded his school, the Garden, in Athens.

Impact

Epicurean philosophy offered a materialist worldview and an ethics based on pleasure and pain that influenced Roman thought, the Scientific Revolution, and modern utilitarian ethics.
