Fyodor Tyutchev

  • Lifespan: 1803 – 1873
  • Fields: Literature
  • Countries: Russia

Overview

Fyodor Tyutchev was a Russian poet and diplomat considered one of the three greatest Russian poets alongside Pushkin and Lermontov. His philosophical lyrics explore nature and the cosmos.

Early Life

Tyutchev was born into a noble family near Bryansk. He spent over 20 years abroad as a Russian diplomat in Munich and Turin before returning to Russia.

Impact

Tyutchev's poetry, combining Romantic intensity with philosophical depth, influenced Russian Symbolism and remains central to the Russian poetic tradition.
