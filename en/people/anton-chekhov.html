Anton Chekhov

  • Lifespan: 1860 – 1904
  • Fields: Literature
  • Countries: Russia

Overview

Anton Chekhov was a Russian playwright and short-story writer regarded as one of the greatest short-story writers in history. His plays revolutionized modern drama.

Early Life

Chekhov was born in Taganrog, Russia. He studied medicine and practiced as a physician while writing, famously saying that medicine was his wife and literature his mistress.

Impact

Chekhov transformed both the short story and modern drama. His plays pioneered the use of subtext and indirect action, profoundly influencing 20th-century theater.
