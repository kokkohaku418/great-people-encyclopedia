Mirabai

  • Lifespan: 1498 – 1546
  • Fields: Literature
  • Countries: India

Overview

Mirabai was a Rajput princess and mystic poet devoted to Lord Krishna, one of the most beloved Bhakti saints.

Early Life

Born into a royal Rajput family in Merta, Rajasthan. Married the crown prince of Mewar.

Impact

Her devotional songs are sung across India to this day and embody the intensity of Bhakti love poetry.
