Homer

  • Lifespan: -800 – -701
  • Fields: Literature
  • Countries: Greece

Overview

Homer was an ancient Greek poet traditionally credited as the author of the Iliad and the Odyssey, the foundational works of Western literature.

Early Life

Almost nothing is known about Homer's life. Ancient traditions describe him as a blind bard from Ionia. Whether he was a single author or represents a tradition remains debated.

Impact

The Iliad and Odyssey established the epic tradition and profoundly influenced all subsequent Western literature, philosophy, and art.
