Suryavarman II

  • Lifespan: 1094 – 1150
  • Fields: Politics
  • Countries: kh

Overview

Suryavarman II was the Khmer king who built Angkor Wat, the largest religious monument in the world.

Early Life

Rose to power by unifying the fractured Khmer kingdom through political and military prowess.

Impact

He created one of humanity's greatest architectural achievements and expanded the Khmer Empire to its greatest extent.
