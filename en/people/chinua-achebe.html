Chinua Achebe

  • Lifespan: 1930 – 2013
  • Fields: Literature
  • Countries: Nigeria

Overview

Chinua Achebe was a Nigerian novelist whose Things Fall Apart is the most widely read book in modern African literature. He is considered the father of African literature in English.

Early Life

Achebe was born in Ogidi, Nigeria. He studied at the University of Ibadan and worked at the Nigerian Broadcasting Corporation before becoming a full-time writer and academic.

Impact

Things Fall Apart challenged Western narratives about Africa and established African fiction as a major literary force. Achebe's work inspired generations of African and postcolonial writers.
