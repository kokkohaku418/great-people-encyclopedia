Søren Kierkegaard

  • Lifespan: 1813 – 1855
  • Fields: Philosophy
  • Countries: Denmark

Overview

Søren Kierkegaard was a Danish philosopher, theologian, and cultural critic, widely considered the first existentialist philosopher.

Early Life

Kierkegaard was born in Copenhagen to a wealthy family. He studied theology at the University of Copenhagen and spent most of his life writing in Copenhagen.

Impact

Kierkegaard's emphasis on individual existence, subjective truth, and the leap of faith founded existentialism and profoundly influenced 20th-century philosophy, theology, and psychology.
