Miguel de Cervantes

  • Lifespan: 1547 – 1616
  • Fields: Literature
  • Countries: Spain

Overview

Miguel de Cervantes was a Spanish novelist, poet, and playwright best known for Don Quixote, which is often considered the first modern novel and one of the greatest works of fiction.

Early Life

Cervantes was born in Alcalá de Henares. He served as a soldier, was captured by pirates and held as a slave in Algiers for five years before being ransomed.

Impact

Don Quixote is considered the first modern novel and one of the most influential works of fiction ever written. Cervantes' blend of comedy and pathos created a new literary form.
