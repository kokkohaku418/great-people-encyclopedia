Ferdinand de Saussure

  • Lifespan: 1857 – 1913
  • Fields: Philosophy
  • Countries: Switzerland

Overview

Ferdinand de Saussure was a Swiss linguist whose ideas on the structure of language founded modern structural linguistics and influenced the entire field of semiotics.

Early Life

Born in Geneva to a prominent scientific family, he published a groundbreaking work on Indo-European vowels at age twenty-one.

Impact

His distinction between langue and parole, the concept of the sign as signifier and signified, and the synchronic approach to language study revolutionized linguistics and influenced structuralism across all humanities.
