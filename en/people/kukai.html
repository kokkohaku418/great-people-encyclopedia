Kukai

  • Lifespan: 774 – 835
  • Fields: Philosophy
  • Countries: Japan

Overview

Kukai was a Japanese Buddhist monk who founded the Shingon school of esoteric Buddhism and is one of Japan's most revered cultural figures.

Early Life

Born on the island of Shikoku, he was a prodigious scholar who studied in China under the esoteric master Huiguo.

Impact

He established Shingon Buddhism, created the kana syllabary that democratized Japanese literacy, and founded the pilgrimage route of the 88 temples of Shikoku.
