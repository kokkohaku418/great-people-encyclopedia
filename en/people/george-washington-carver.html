George Washington Carver

  • Lifespan: 1864 – 1943
  • Fields: Biology, Chemistry
  • Countries: United States

Overview

George Washington Carver was an American agricultural scientist who developed hundreds of products from peanuts, sweet potatoes, and soybeans.

Early Life

Born into slavery in Missouri. Studied at Iowa State Agricultural College.

Impact

Carver's agricultural research helped poor Southern farmers diversify their crops and improve soil depleted by cotton farming.
