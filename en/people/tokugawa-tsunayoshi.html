Tokugawa Tsunayoshi

  • Lifespan: 1646 – 1709
  • Fields: Politics
  • Countries: Japan

Overview

Tokugawa Tsunayoshi was the fifth shogun of the Tokugawa dynasty, known for the Laws of Compassion for Living Things.

Early Life

Born in Edo as the fourth son of Tokugawa Iemitsu.

Impact

His reign saw both cultural flourishing and controversial animal protection laws.
