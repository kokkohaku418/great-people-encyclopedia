Jose de San Martin

  • Lifespan: 1778 – 1850
  • Fields: Politics
  • Countries: Argentina

Overview

Jose de San Martin was an Argentine general who led the liberation of Argentina, Chile, and Peru from Spanish rule, earning the title 'Liberator of the South.'

Early Life

Born in Yapeyu in the Viceroyalty of the Rio de la Plata, he was trained as a military officer in Spain before returning to South America to fight for independence.

Impact

He is venerated as the national hero of Argentina, Chile, and Peru for his role in their independence from Spain.
