Samuel Morse

  • Lifespan: 1791 – 1872
  • Fields: Engineering
  • Countries: United States

Overview

Samuel Morse was an American inventor and painter who developed the electric telegraph and the Morse code, revolutionizing long-distance communication.

Early Life

Born in Charlestown, Massachusetts, he was initially a successful portrait painter who became interested in electromagnetism after a personal tragedy during which delayed news of his wife's death arrived too late.

Impact

The telegraph system he developed transformed global communication, enabling near-instantaneous long-distance messaging and laying the groundwork for modern telecommunications.
