Johann Wolfgang von Goethe

  • Lifespan: 1749 – 1832
  • Fields: Literature, Philosophy
  • Countries: Germany

Overview

Goethe was a German writer and polymath whose works span poetry, drama, literature, philosophy, and science. He is considered the greatest German literary figure.

Early Life

Goethe was born in Frankfurt am Main to a prosperous family. He studied law at Leipzig and Strasbourg before gaining fame with his novel The Sorrows of Young Werther.

Impact

Goethe's Faust is one of the greatest works of Western literature. His influence extends beyond literature to philosophy, science, and art, and he shaped German cultural identity.
