Nikolaus Otto

  • Lifespan: 1832 – 1891
  • Fields: Engineering
  • Countries: Germany

Overview

Nikolaus Otto was a German engineer who developed the first practical internal combustion engine using the four-stroke cycle that still powers most cars today.

Early Life

Born in Holzhausen, he was self-educated and worked as a traveling salesman before becoming obsessed with building a gas-powered engine.

Impact

The four-stroke Otto cycle engine became the basis for nearly all internal combustion engines, making possible the automobile revolution.
