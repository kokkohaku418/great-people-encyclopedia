Virgil

  • Lifespan: -70 – -19
  • Fields: Literature
  • Countries: Italy

Overview

Virgil was an ancient Roman poet, best known for the Aeneid, the epic poem that tells the story of Rome's founding and became the definitive literary work of the Roman Empire.

Early Life

Virgil was born near Mantua in Cisalpine Gaul. He studied in Cremona, Milan, and Rome, and gained fame with his Eclogues before becoming Rome's national poet.

Impact

The Aeneid became the foundational myth of Rome and one of the most influential poems in Western literature. Virgil's influence extends through Dante, Milton, and all of Western literary tradition.
