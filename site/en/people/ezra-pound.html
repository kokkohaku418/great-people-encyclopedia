Ezra Pound

  • Lifespan: 1885 – 1972
  • Fields: Literature
  • Countries: United States

Overview

Ezra Pound was an expatriate American poet and critic who was a driving force behind literary modernism.

Early Life

Born in Hailey, Idaho. Studied at Hamilton College and the University of Pennsylvania.

Impact

He shaped modernist poetry through the Imagist movement and his editorial influence on Eliot and other poets.
