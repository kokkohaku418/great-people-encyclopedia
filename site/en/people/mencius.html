Mencius

  • Lifespan: -372 – -289
  • Fields: Philosophy
  • Countries: China

Overview

Mencius was a Chinese Confucian philosopher often described as the second sage of Confucianism. He argued that human nature is innately good and that rulers must govern benevolently.

Early Life

Mencius was born in the state of Zou, near Confucius's home state of Lu. He studied under a student of Confucius's grandson and traveled among the Warring States rulers.

Impact

Mencius's arguments for innate human goodness and benevolent government became central to Confucian thought and East Asian political philosophy.
