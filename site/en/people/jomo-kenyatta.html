Jomo Kenyatta

  • Lifespan: 1897 – 1978
  • Fields: Politics
  • Countries: Kenya

Overview

Jomo Kenyatta was the first Prime Minister and President of Kenya, a leader of the independence movement.

Early Life

Born in Gatundu, Kenya. Studied at the London School of Economics.

Impact

Kenyatta led Kenya to independence and shaped its early development as a nation.
