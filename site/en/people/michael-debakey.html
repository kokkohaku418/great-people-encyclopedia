Michael DeBakey

  • Lifespan: 1908 – 2008
  • Fields: Biology, Engineering
  • Countries: United States

Overview

Michael DeBakey was an American cardiac surgeon and medical inventor who pioneered many cardiovascular surgical procedures and devices.

Early Life

Born in Lake Charles, Louisiana, to Lebanese immigrant parents, he studied at Tulane University and developed a roller pump as a medical student that later became a key component of the heart-lung machine.

Impact

He performed the first successful carotid endarterectomy, developed artificial hearts and ventricular assist devices, and trained two generations of cardiac surgeons.
