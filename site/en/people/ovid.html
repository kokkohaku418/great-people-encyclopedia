Ovid

  • Lifespan: -43 – 17
  • Fields: Literature
  • Countries: Italy

Overview

Ovid was a Roman poet whose Metamorphoses, a narrative poem about transformations in Greek and Roman mythology, is one of the most influential works in Western literature.

Early Life

Ovid was born in Sulmo, east of Rome. He studied rhetoric in Rome and traveled to Athens before devoting himself to poetry. Emperor Augustus exiled him to the Black Sea in 8 AD.

Impact

Ovid's Metamorphoses shaped Western art, literature, and culture more than almost any other classical work. Shakespeare, Dante, and countless artists drew directly from his mythological narratives.
