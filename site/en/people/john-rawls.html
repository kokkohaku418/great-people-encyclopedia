John Rawls

  • Lifespan: 1921 – 2002
  • Fields: Philosophy, Politics
  • Countries: United States

Overview

John Rawls was an American political philosopher whose A Theory of Justice is one of the most influential works of political philosophy in the 20th century.

Early Life

Rawls was born in Baltimore, Maryland. He served in the Pacific during World War II and later earned his PhD from Princeton, eventually becoming professor at Harvard.

Impact

Rawls's theory of justice as fairness, his veil of ignorance thought experiment, and his concept of the original position revived social contract theory and shaped contemporary liberal political philosophy.
