William Shakespeare

  • Lifespan: 1564 – 1616
  • Fields: Literature
  • Countries: United Kingdom

Overview

William Shakespeare was an English playwright and poet, widely regarded as the greatest writer in the English language. His plays have been translated into every major language and are performed more often than those of any other playwright.

Early Life

Shakespeare was born in Stratford-upon-Avon. He married Anne Hathaway at 18 and moved to London, where he became an actor and playwright with the Lord Chamberlain's Men.

Impact

Shakespeare's works defined the English literary canon and profoundly influenced world literature, theater, and the English language itself. He invented over 1,700 words still in use today.
