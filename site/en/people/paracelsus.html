Paracelsus

  • Lifespan: 1493 – 1541
  • Fields: Chemistry, Biology
  • Countries: Switzerland

Overview

Paracelsus was a Swiss-German physician and alchemist who pioneered the use of chemicals and minerals in medicine, founding the field of toxicology.

Early Life

Born Theophrastus von Hohenheim near Zurich, he wandered across Europe learning folk medicine and alchemy before challenging the medical establishment.

Impact

He revolutionized medicine by introducing chemical remedies, establishing the dose-response principle of toxicology, and challenging the ancient authority of Galen.
