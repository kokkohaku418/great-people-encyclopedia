Uesugi Kenshin

  • Lifespan: 1530 – 1578
  • Fields: Politics
  • Countries: Japan

Overview

Uesugi Kenshin was a daimyo of Echigo Province renowned as the 'God of War' and the legendary rival of Takeda Shingen during Japan's Sengoku period.

Early Life

Born Nagao Kagetora, he took control of Echigo Province at a young age and was a devout follower of Bishamonten, the Buddhist god of war.

Impact

He is celebrated as one of the most skilled military commanders in Japanese history, known for his honorable conduct in warfare.
