Thomas More

  • Lifespan: 1478 – 1535
  • Fields: Philosophy, Politics
  • Countries: United Kingdom

Overview

Thomas More was an English lawyer, philosopher, and statesman who served as Lord Chancellor under Henry VIII. His Utopia described an ideal society on a fictional island.

Early Life

More was born in London and studied law at Lincoln's Inn. He rose through political ranks while maintaining his humanist scholarship and friendship with Erasmus.

Impact

More's Utopia coined a word and genre for imagined ideal societies. His martyrdom for refusing to accept Henry VIII's supremacy over the Church made him a saint of the Catholic Church.
