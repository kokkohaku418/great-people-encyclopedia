Avicenna (Medical)

  • Lifespan: 980 – 1037
  • Fields: Biology
  • Countries: Iran

Overview

Avicenna wrote the Canon of Medicine, the most influential medical textbook for centuries.

Early Life

Born near Bukhara in present-day Uzbekistan.

Impact

The Canon of Medicine was used as a standard text in European universities until the 17th century.
