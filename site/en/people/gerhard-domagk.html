Gerhard Domagk

  • Lifespan: 1895 – 1964
  • Fields: Biology, Chemistry
  • Countries: Germany

Overview

Gerhard Domagk was a German pathologist and bacteriologist who discovered sulfonamide, the first commercially available antibiotic, saving millions of lives.

Early Life

Born in Lagow, Brandenburg, he served as a medic in World War I and was profoundly affected by the infections that killed wounded soldiers.

Impact

His discovery of Prontosil launched the sulfonamide drug revolution that was the first effective treatment for bacterial infections, preceding penicillin.
