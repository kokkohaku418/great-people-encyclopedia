Charles Baudelaire

  • Lifespan: 1821 – 1867
  • Fields: Literature
  • Countries: France

Overview

Charles Baudelaire was a French poet who pioneered symbolism and modern poetry.

Early Life

Born in Paris. Lost his father at age six.

Impact

His work profoundly influenced the direction of modern poetry.
