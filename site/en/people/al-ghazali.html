Al-Ghazali

  • Lifespan: 1058 – 1111
  • Fields: Philosophy
  • Countries: Iran

Overview

Al-Ghazali was a Persian theologian, philosopher, and mystic who profoundly influenced Islamic thought. His critique of philosophy and embrace of Sufism reshaped the Islamic intellectual tradition.

Early Life

Al-Ghazali was born in Tus, Persia. He became a professor at the Nizamiyyah university in Baghdad before experiencing a spiritual crisis that led him to abandon his position for Sufi practice.

Impact

Al-Ghazali's The Incoherence of the Philosophers challenged rationalist philosophy in Islam, while his Revival of the Religious Sciences synthesized orthodox theology with Sufi mysticism.
