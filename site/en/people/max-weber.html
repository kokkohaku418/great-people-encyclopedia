Max Weber

  • Lifespan: 1864 – 1920
  • Fields: Philosophy
  • Countries: Germany

Overview

Max Weber was a German sociologist and political economist whose ideas on bureaucracy, authority, and the Protestant ethic profoundly shaped modern social science.

Early Life

Born in Erfurt to a wealthy and politically active family, he studied law and history and became a professor before suffering a severe mental breakdown.

Impact

His concept of the 'iron cage' of bureaucracy, ideal types methodology, and analysis of the relationship between religion and capitalism became foundational to sociology.
