Selman Waksman

  • Lifespan: 1888 – 1973
  • Fields: Biology
  • Countries: Ukraine, United States

Overview

Selman Waksman was a Ukrainian-American microbiologist who discovered streptomycin, the first antibiotic effective against tuberculosis.

Early Life

Born in Novaya Priluka, Ukraine, he emigrated to the United States and studied soil microbiology at Rutgers University.

Impact

His systematic search for antibiotics in soil microorganisms led to the discovery of streptomycin, saving millions from tuberculosis and coining the word 'antibiotic.'
