Émile Picard

  • Lifespan: 1856 – 1941
  • Fields: Mathematics
  • Countries: France

Overview

Émile Picard made important contributions to analysis and algebraic geometry.

Early Life

Born in Paris. Studied at École Normale Supérieure.

Impact

His theorems on entire functions are fundamental in complex analysis.
