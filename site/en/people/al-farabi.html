Al-Farabi

  • Lifespan: 872 – 950
  • Fields: Philosophy
  • Countries: kz

Overview

Al-Farabi was a renowned Islamic philosopher and polymath known as the Second Teacher after Aristotle. He made major contributions to political philosophy, logic, and music theory.

Early Life

Al-Farabi was born in Farab, in present-day Kazakhstan. He studied in Baghdad, the intellectual center of the Islamic Golden Age.

Impact

Al-Farabi's commentaries on Aristotle and Plato preserved and transmitted Greek philosophy to the medieval world. His political philosophy influenced both Islamic and European thought.
