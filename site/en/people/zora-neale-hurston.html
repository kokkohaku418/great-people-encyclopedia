Zora Neale Hurston

  • Lifespan: 1891 – 1960
  • Fields: Literature
  • Countries: United States

Overview

Zora Neale Hurston was a novelist and anthropologist who celebrated Black Southern culture.

Early Life

Born in Notasulga, Alabama and raised in Eatonville, Florida, one of the first all-Black towns in America.

Impact

Her masterpiece Their Eyes Were Watching God was rediscovered through Alice Walker's advocacy and became a classic.
