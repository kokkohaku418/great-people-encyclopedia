Duns Scotus

  • Lifespan: 1266 – 1308
  • Fields: Philosophy
  • Countries: United Kingdom

Overview

Duns Scotus was a Franciscan philosopher-theologian known for his subtle and rigorous arguments.

Early Life

Born in Duns, Scotland. Studied and taught at Oxford, Paris, and Cologne.

Impact

His concept of haecceity (individual essence) and univocity of being were major contributions to metaphysics.
