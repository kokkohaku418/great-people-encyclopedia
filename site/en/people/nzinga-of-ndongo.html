Nzinga of Ndongo

  • Lifespan: 1583 – 1663
  • Fields: Politics
  • Countries: ao

Overview

Queen Nzinga was a fearless ruler of the Ndongo and Matamba kingdoms in present-day Angola who fiercely resisted Portuguese colonialism.

Early Life

Born a princess of the Ndongo kingdom, she served as a diplomat for her brother before seizing power and becoming queen.

Impact

She fought the Portuguese for over three decades, forming alliances with the Dutch and neighboring kingdoms, and became an enduring symbol of African resistance.
