Dom Pedro I

  • Lifespan: 1798 – 1834
  • Fields: Politics
  • Countries: Brazil

Overview

Dom Pedro I was the founder and first Emperor of Brazil who declared the country's independence from Portugal in 1822.

Early Life

Born in Lisbon as the son of King Joao VI of Portugal, he came to Brazil as a child when the royal family fled Napoleon's invasion.

Impact

He declared Brazilian independence with the famous 'Cry of Ipiranga' and established Brazil as an independent constitutional monarchy.
