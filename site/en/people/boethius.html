Boethius

  • Lifespan: 480 – 524
  • Fields: Philosophy
  • Countries: Italy

Overview

Boethius wrote The Consolation of Philosophy, one of the most widely read books of the Middle Ages.

Early Life

Born in Rome to an aristocratic family. Served as consul and magister officiorum under the Ostrogothic king Theodoric.

Impact

He transmitted Greek philosophy to the medieval Latin West and wrote the most influential philosophical work of the early Middle Ages.
