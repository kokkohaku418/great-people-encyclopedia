Rumi

  • Lifespan: 1207 – 1273
  • Fields: Literature, Philosophy
  • Countries: af, Turkey

Overview

Rumi was a 13th-century Persian poet, Sufi mystic, and Islamic scholar whose poetry of divine love is among the most widely read in the world. He is the best-selling poet in the United States.

Early Life

Rumi was born in Balkh, in present-day Afghanistan. His family fled the Mongol invasions and settled in Konya, in present-day Turkey, where Rumi became a scholar and teacher.

Impact

Rumi's mystical poetry transcends cultural and religious boundaries. The Masnavi is considered one of the greatest poems in the Persian language, and his works continue to inspire millions worldwide.
