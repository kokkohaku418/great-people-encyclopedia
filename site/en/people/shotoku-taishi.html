Shotoku Taishi

  • Lifespan: 574 – 622
  • Fields: Politics
  • Countries: Japan

Overview

Prince Shotoku was a semi-legendary regent of Japan who promoted Buddhism and Chinese political institutions, shaping the foundations of Japanese civilization.

Early Life

Born as Prince Umayado, he served as regent for Empress Suiko and was renowned for his intelligence and piety.

Impact

He established Buddhism as a state religion, introduced the Chinese calendar and court ranks, and authored Japan's first constitution.
