Mary Wollstonecraft

  • Lifespan: 1759 – 1797
  • Fields: Philosophy, Politics
  • Countries: United Kingdom

Overview

Mary Wollstonecraft was a British writer and philosopher who is considered the founder of feminist philosophy. Her Vindication of the Rights of Woman argued for women's equality.

Early Life

Wollstonecraft was born in London to a family of declining means. Largely self-educated, she worked as a governess and teacher before becoming a writer and political commentator.

Impact

Wollstonecraft's Vindication of the Rights of Woman is a foundational text of feminism. Her arguments for women's education and equality influenced the suffragist movements and modern gender equality.
