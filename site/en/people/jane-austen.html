Jane Austen

  • Lifespan: 1775 – 1817
  • Fields: Literature
  • Countries: United Kingdom

Overview

Jane Austen was an English novelist known for her wit, social commentary, and masterful depiction of British landed gentry. Her novels remain among the most widely read in English literature.

Early Life

Austen was born in Steventon, Hampshire, to a large family. She began writing as a teenager and lived a quiet life in the English countryside, never marrying.

Impact

Austen's novels transformed the English novel through their irony, realism, and psychological depth. Her works pioneered free indirect discourse and influenced countless writers.
