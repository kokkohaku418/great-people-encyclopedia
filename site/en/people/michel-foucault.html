Michel Foucault

  • Lifespan: 1926 – 1984
  • Fields: Philosophy
  • Countries: France

Overview

Michel Foucault was a French philosopher and social theorist whose analyses of power, knowledge, and discourse transformed the humanities and social sciences.

Early Life

Foucault was born in Poitiers, France. He studied at the École Normale Supérieure and earned degrees in philosophy and psychology.

Impact

Foucault's analyses of how power operates through institutions, discourse, and knowledge reshaped sociology, cultural studies, literary theory, and political thought.
