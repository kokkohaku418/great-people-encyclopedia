Socrates

  • Lifespan: -470 – -399
  • Fields: Philosophy
  • Countries: Greece

Overview

Socrates was an ancient Greek philosopher credited as the founder of Western philosophy. He developed the Socratic method of questioning to stimulate critical thinking.

Early Life

Socrates was born in Athens. His father was a stonemason and his mother a midwife. He served in the Athenian military and later devoted himself entirely to philosophical inquiry.

Impact

Socrates' method of dialectical questioning became the foundation of Western philosophical inquiry. His emphasis on ethics, self-knowledge, and reasoned argument transformed intellectual life.
