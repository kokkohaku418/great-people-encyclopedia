Dante Alighieri

  • Lifespan: 1265 – 1321
  • Fields: Literature
  • Countries: Italy

Overview

Dante Alighieri was an Italian poet whose Divine Comedy is considered one of the greatest works of world literature and the preeminent work of Italian literature.

Early Life

Dante was born in Florence to a family of moderate wealth. He was deeply involved in Florentine politics and was exiled from the city in 1302, never to return.

Impact

The Divine Comedy established Italian as a literary language and profoundly influenced Western literature, theology, and art. Its vision of the afterlife shaped European imagination for centuries.
