Nicholas of Cusa

  • Lifespan: 1401 – 1464
  • Fields: Philosophy
  • Countries: Germany

Overview

Nicholas of Cusa was a philosopher, theologian, and cardinal who anticipated many modern ideas.

Early Life

Born in Kues on the Moselle River. Studied at Heidelberg, Padua, and Cologne.

Impact

His concept of learned ignorance and the coincidence of opposites influenced later philosophy and theology.
