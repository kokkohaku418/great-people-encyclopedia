Sen no Rikyu

  • Lifespan: 1522 – 1591
  • Fields: Art
  • Countries: Japan

Overview

Sen no Rikyu was the most influential figure in the Japanese tea ceremony, perfecting the wabi-cha aesthetic of simplicity and rusticity.

Early Life

Born in Sakai to a wealthy merchant family, he studied tea under Takeno Joo and became tea master to both Oda Nobunaga and Toyotomi Hideyoshi.

Impact

He transformed the tea ceremony into a profound artistic and philosophical practice that influenced Japanese aesthetics, architecture, and ceramics for centuries.
