Bernardo O'Higgins

  • Lifespan: 1778 – 1842
  • Fields: Politics
  • Countries: Chile

Overview

Bernardo O'Higgins was the Chilean independence leader and first Supreme Director of Chile, known as the 'Father of the Nation.'

Early Life

Born the illegitimate son of the Irish-born Spanish colonial governor Ambrosio O'Higgins, he was educated in England where he was influenced by Francisco de Miranda's independence ideas.

Impact

He led Chile to independence from Spain and established the foundations of the Chilean state, including its first constitution and navy.
