Augustine of Hippo

  • Lifespan: 354 – 430
  • Fields: Philosophy
  • Countries: dz

Overview

Augustine of Hippo was a theologian and philosopher who profoundly shaped Western Christianity and philosophy. His Confessions is considered the first Western autobiography.

Early Life

Augustine was born in Thagaste, Roman North Africa. He lived a dissolute youth before his dramatic conversion to Christianity in 386 AD, influenced by Ambrose of Milan.

Impact

Augustine's theology of grace, original sin, and the City of God dominated Western Christian thought for a millennium. His introspective Confessions pioneered autobiography and the philosophy of time.
