Denis Diderot

  • Lifespan: 1713 – 1784
  • Fields: Philosophy
  • Countries: France

Overview

Denis Diderot was a French Enlightenment philosopher and chief editor of the Encyclopedie, one of the most ambitious intellectual projects in history.

Early Life

Born in Langres to a master cutler, he was educated by the Jesuits and spent years as a struggling writer in Paris before embarking on the Encyclopedie.

Impact

The Encyclopedie he co-edited became the defining work of the Enlightenment, democratizing knowledge and challenging religious and political authority.
