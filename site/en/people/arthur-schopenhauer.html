Arthur Schopenhauer

  • Lifespan: 1788 – 1860
  • Fields: Philosophy
  • Countries: Germany

Overview

Arthur Schopenhauer was a German philosopher best known for his work The World as Will and Representation, which characterized the phenomenal world as driven by a blind, purposeless will.

Early Life

Schopenhauer was born in Danzig to a wealthy merchant family. He studied philosophy at the University of Berlin, where he developed a rivalry with Hegel.

Impact

Schopenhauer's pessimistic philosophy and his emphasis on the will influenced Nietzsche, Freud, Wagner, Wittgenstein, and existentialism. He was also one of the first Western philosophers to engage seriously with Eastern thought.
