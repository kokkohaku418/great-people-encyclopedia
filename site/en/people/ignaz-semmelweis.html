Ignaz Semmelweis

  • Lifespan: 1818 – 1865
  • Fields: Biology
  • Countries: Hungary

Overview

Ignaz Semmelweis was a Hungarian physician who discovered that handwashing by doctors dramatically reduced the incidence of childbed fever, pioneering antiseptic medicine.

Early Life

Born in Buda to a German-speaking family, he studied medicine in Vienna and became an assistant at the Vienna General Hospital's maternity clinic.

Impact

His discovery that hand disinfection could prevent puerperal fever saved countless lives, though his ideas were rejected during his lifetime in one of medicine's greatest tragedies.
