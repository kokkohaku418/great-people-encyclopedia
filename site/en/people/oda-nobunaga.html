Oda Nobunaga

  • Lifespan: 1534 – 1582
  • Fields: Politics
  • Countries: Japan

Overview

Oda Nobunaga was the first of the three great unifiers of Japan who began the process of ending the Sengoku period of civil war.

Early Life

Born in Owari Province to a minor daimyo family, he was known as the 'Fool of Owari' in his youth for his eccentric behavior.

Impact

He revolutionized Japanese warfare with firearms, destroyed the political power of Buddhist monasteries, and laid the groundwork for Japan's unification.

Timeline
