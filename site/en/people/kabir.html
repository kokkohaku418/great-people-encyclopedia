Kabir

  • Lifespan: 1398 – 1518
  • Fields: Literature, Philosophy
  • Countries: India

Overview

Kabir was a mystic poet-saint whose verses transcended Hindu and Muslim boundaries.

Early Life

Born in Varanasi, likely into a Muslim weaver family. Influenced by both Hindu and Sufi traditions.

Impact

His poetry profoundly influenced the Bhakti movement and remains central to Indian spiritual literature.
