Marcus Aurelius

  • Lifespan: 121 – 180
  • Fields: Philosophy, Politics
  • Countries: Italy

Overview

Marcus Aurelius was a Roman emperor and Stoic philosopher whose Meditations is one of the greatest works of Stoic philosophy, written as private reflections during military campaigns.

Early Life

Marcus Aurelius was born in Rome to a prominent family. Adopted by Emperor Antoninus Pius, he received an excellent education including training in Stoic philosophy.

Impact

The Meditations remains one of the most widely read philosophical works, offering timeless guidance on duty, virtue, and resilience. Marcus Aurelius is considered the last of the Five Good Emperors.
