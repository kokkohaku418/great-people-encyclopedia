Alexis de Tocqueville

  • Lifespan: 1805 – 1859
  • Fields: Philosophy, Politics
  • Countries: France

Overview

Alexis de Tocqueville was a French political thinker and historian whose Democracy in America remains the most penetrating analysis of American democratic society ever written.

Early Life

Born into a Norman aristocratic family, he traveled to America in 1831 ostensibly to study the prison system but used the trip to examine American democracy broadly.

Impact

His insights on democracy, equality, civic association, and the tyranny of the majority remain essential to political science and democratic theory worldwide.
