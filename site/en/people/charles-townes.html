Charles Townes

  • Lifespan: 1915 – 2015
  • Fields: Physics
  • Countries: United States

Overview

Charles Townes invented the maser and contributed fundamental work leading to the laser.

Early Life

Born in Greenville, South Carolina. Studied at Furman University and Caltech.

Impact

His invention of the maser and theoretical work on the laser transformed communications, medicine, and science.
