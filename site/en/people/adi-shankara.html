Adi Shankara

  • Lifespan: 788 – 820
  • Fields: Philosophy
  • Countries: India

Overview

Adi Shankara was an Indian philosopher who consolidated the doctrine of Advaita Vedanta (non-dualism).

Early Life

Born in Kaladi, Kerala. Became a sannyasi at a very young age and traveled across India.

Impact

He revitalized Hindu philosophy, established four mathas (monasteries), and is one of India's greatest thinkers.
