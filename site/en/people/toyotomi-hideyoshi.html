Toyotomi Hideyoshi

  • Lifespan: 1537 – 1598
  • Fields: Politics
  • Countries: Japan

Overview

Toyotomi Hideyoshi rose from peasant origins to become the second great unifier of Japan, completing the work Oda Nobunaga had begun.

Early Life

Born to a poor peasant family in Owari Province, he entered Nobunaga's service as a sandal-bearer and rose through merit to become a top general.

Impact

He unified all of Japan, implemented the land survey and sword hunt that solidified the class system, and launched ambitious but ultimately failed invasions of Korea.

  • 1592 — Launched the first invasion of Korea
