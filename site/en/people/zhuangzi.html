Zhuangzi

  • Lifespan: -369 – -286
  • Fields: Philosophy
  • Countries: China

Overview

Zhuangzi was an ancient Chinese philosopher and one of the founders of Taoism. His eponymous book is one of the most important texts in Chinese philosophy.

Early Life

Little is known about Zhuangzi's life. He is traditionally believed to have lived in the state of Meng during the Warring States period and declined a government position.

Impact

Zhuangzi's philosophy of spontaneity, relativism, and harmony with nature profoundly influenced Chinese thought, art, poetry, and Chan/Zen Buddhism.
