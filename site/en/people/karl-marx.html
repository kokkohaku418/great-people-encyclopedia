Karl Marx

  • Lifespan: 1818 – 1883
  • Fields: Philosophy, Politics
  • Countries: Germany, United Kingdom

Overview

Karl Marx was a German philosopher, economist, and revolutionary socialist whose works on capitalism, class struggle, and historical materialism shaped modern political thought and world history.

Early Life

Marx was born in Trier, Prussia. He studied law and philosophy at the Universities of Bonn and Berlin, where he was influenced by Hegel's philosophy.

Impact

Marx's analysis of capitalism, theory of historical materialism, and call for proletarian revolution influenced revolutions worldwide and shaped the political landscape of the 20th century.
