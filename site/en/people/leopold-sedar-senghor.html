Léopold Sédar Senghor

  • Lifespan: 1906 – 2001
  • Fields: Politics, Literature
  • Countries: sn

Overview

Léopold Sédar Senghor was the first President of Senegal and a major poet who co-founded the Négritude literary movement.

Early Life

Born in Joal, Senegal. Studied at the Sorbonne in Paris.

Impact

Senghor's Négritude movement celebrated African culture and identity, influencing decolonization across Africa.
