Suharto

  • Lifespan: 1921 – 2008
  • Fields: Politics
  • Countries: Indonesia

Overview

Suharto was the second president of Indonesia, ruling for 31 years during the New Order era.

Early Life

Born in Kemusuk, Java. Rose through the ranks of the Indonesian military.

Impact

He oversaw significant economic development but also widespread corruption and human rights abuses.
