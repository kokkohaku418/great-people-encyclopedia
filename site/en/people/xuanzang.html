Xuanzang

  • Lifespan: 602 – 664
  • Fields: Philosophy
  • Countries: China

Overview

Xuanzang was a Chinese Buddhist monk who undertook an epic seventeen-year journey to India to obtain sacred Buddhist texts.

Early Life

Born in Luoyang, Henan, he became a monk at age thirteen and grew dissatisfied with the incomplete Chinese Buddhist scriptures.

Impact

His translations of Sanskrit texts transformed Chinese Buddhism, and his travel account inspired the classic novel Journey to the West.
