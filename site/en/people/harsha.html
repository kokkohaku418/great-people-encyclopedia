Harsha

  • Lifespan: 590 – 647
  • Fields: Politics, Literature
  • Countries: India

Overview

Emperor Harsha of Kannauj was one of the last great rulers of ancient India, known for his patronage of arts and Buddhism.

Early Life

Born into the Pushyabhuti dynasty. Became king at age 16 after the deaths of his father and brother.

Impact

He unified much of northern India and promoted religious tolerance and learning.
