Epictetus

  • Lifespan: 50 – 135
  • Fields: Philosophy
  • Countries: Greece, Italy

Overview

Epictetus was a Greek Stoic philosopher who was born a slave and became one of the most influential teachers of Stoic ethics in the Roman Empire.

Early Life

Epictetus was born a slave in Hierapolis, Phrygia. After gaining his freedom, he studied under the Stoic philosopher Musonius Rufus and later founded his own school in Nicopolis.

Impact

Epictetus's Discourses and Enchiridion became foundational Stoic texts emphasizing that virtue and happiness depend on what is within our control. His influence extends to cognitive behavioral therapy.
