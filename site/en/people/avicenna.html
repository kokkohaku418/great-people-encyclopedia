Avicenna

  • Lifespan: 980 – 1037
  • Fields: Philosophy, Biology
  • Countries: Iran

Overview

Avicenna (Ibn Sina) was a Persian polymath regarded as one of the most significant thinkers of the Islamic Golden Age. He made major contributions to philosophy, medicine, and science.

Early Life

Avicenna was born near Bukhara in present-day Uzbekistan. A child prodigy, he had memorized the Quran by age 10 and mastered medicine by 18.

Impact

Avicenna's Canon of Medicine was the standard medical textbook in Europe and the Islamic world for centuries. His philosophical works profoundly influenced both Islamic and Scholastic philosophy.
