Michel de Montaigne

  • Lifespan: 1533 – 1592
  • Fields: Philosophy, Literature
  • Countries: France

Overview

Michel de Montaigne was a French philosopher and essayist who popularized the essay as a literary form. His candid explorations of the human condition remain remarkably modern.

Early Life

Montaigne was born in the Périgord region of France to a wealthy family. He was educated in Latin from infancy and later studied law before retiring to write.

Impact

Montaigne's Essays pioneered the personal essay form and influenced Shakespeare, Descartes, Pascal, and Nietzsche. His skeptical humanism and self-examination remain profoundly influential.
