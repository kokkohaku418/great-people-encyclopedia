Gottlieb Daimler

  • Lifespan: 1834 – 1900
  • Fields: Engineering
  • Countries: Germany

Overview

Gottlieb Daimler was a German engineer and industrialist who pioneered the high-speed internal combustion engine and the modern automobile.

Early Life

Born in Schorndorf, Wurttemberg, he trained as a gunsmith and engineer before working with Nikolaus Otto on gas engines and later partnering with Wilhelm Maybach.

Impact

His high-speed petrol engine powered the first motorcycle and one of the first automobiles, and his company eventually merged with Benz to form Daimler-Benz.
