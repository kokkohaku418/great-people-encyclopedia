Cao Xueqin

  • Lifespan: 1715 – 1763
  • Fields: Literature
  • Countries: China

Overview

Cao Xueqin was a Chinese novelist and author of Dream of the Red Chamber, one of China's Four Great Classical Novels and one of the greatest novels in world literature.

Early Life

Cao Xueqin was born into a wealthy family that had served as bondservants to the Qing imperial house. The family's decline from wealth to poverty deeply influenced his masterwork.

Impact

Dream of the Red Chamber is considered the pinnacle of Chinese fiction, admired for its psychological depth, social realism, and lyrical beauty. An entire field of study, Redology, is devoted to it.
