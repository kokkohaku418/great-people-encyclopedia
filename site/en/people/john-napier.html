John Napier

  • Lifespan: 1550 – 1617
  • Fields: Mathematics
  • Countries: United Kingdom

Overview

John Napier invented logarithms, one of the most useful mathematical tools for calculation.

Early Life

Born in Edinburgh. Studied at the University of St Andrews.

Impact

Logarithms transformed calculation and paved the way for modern computation.
