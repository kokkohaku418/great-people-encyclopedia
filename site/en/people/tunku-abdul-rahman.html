Tunku Abdul Rahman

  • Lifespan: 1903 – 1990
  • Fields: Politics
  • Countries: Malaysia

Overview

Tunku Abdul Rahman was the first Prime Minister of Malaya and later Malaysia, known as the Father of Independence.

Early Life

Born in Alor Setar, Kedah. Studied at Cambridge.

Impact

Tunku negotiated Malaya's independence from Britain and formed the Federation of Malaysia.
