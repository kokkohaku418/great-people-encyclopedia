Galen

  • Lifespan: 129 – 216
  • Fields: Biology
  • Countries: Greece

Overview

Galen was a Greek physician and philosopher whose medical theories dominated Western medicine for over 1,300 years.

Early Life

Born in Pergamon to a wealthy architect father, he studied medicine across the Greek world and became physician to the Roman gladiators.

Impact

His anatomical writings and medical theories were the unchallenged authority in medicine from antiquity through the Renaissance.
