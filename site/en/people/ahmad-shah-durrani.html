Ahmad Shah Durrani

  • Lifespan: 1722 – 1772
  • Fields: Politics
  • Countries: af

Overview

Ahmad Shah Durrani was the founder of the Durrani Empire and is considered the father of modern Afghanistan.

Early Life

Born in Multan to an Afghan chief of the Abdali tribe, he served as a cavalry commander under Nader Shah of Persia.

Impact

He united the Pashtun tribes into a single polity that became the foundation of the modern Afghan state and built one of the largest empires of the 18th century.
