Gabriel García Márquez

  • Lifespan: 1927 – 2014
  • Fields: Literature
  • Countries: co

Overview

Gabriel García Márquez was a Colombian novelist and Nobel laureate who was the foremost practitioner of magical realism. One Hundred Years of Solitude is one of the most significant novels of the 20th century.

Early Life

García Márquez was born in Aracataca, Colombia. Raised by his grandparents, he studied law before becoming a journalist and writer.

Impact

García Márquez's magical realism transformed world literature and brought Latin American fiction to global prominence. One Hundred Years of Solitude has sold over 50 million copies.
