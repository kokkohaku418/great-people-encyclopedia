Jiddu Krishnamurti

  • Lifespan: 1895 – 1986
  • Fields: Philosophy
  • Countries: India, United States

Overview

Jiddu Krishnamurti was an Indian-born philosopher and speaker who rejected all organized religion, nationality, and ideology, advocating self-knowledge through direct observation of the mind.

Early Life

Krishnamurti was born in Madanapalle, India. He was adopted by the Theosophical Society, who proclaimed him a World Teacher, a role he later repudiated.

Impact

Krishnamurti's teachings on freedom from conditioning, the nature of the mind, and the importance of self-inquiry influenced millions worldwide and continue through his educational foundations.
