Benazir Bhutto

  • Lifespan: 1953 – 2007
  • Fields: Politics
  • Countries: Pakistan

Overview

Benazir Bhutto was the first woman to head a democratic government in a Muslim-majority country.

Early Life

Born in Karachi. Daughter of Zulfikar Ali Bhutto. Studied at Harvard and Oxford.

Impact

She broke barriers as a female leader in the Muslim world and fought for democracy in Pakistan.
