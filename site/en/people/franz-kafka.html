Franz Kafka

  • Lifespan: 1883 – 1924
  • Fields: Literature
  • Countries: Czech Republic, Austria

Overview

Franz Kafka was a German-speaking Bohemian novelist whose surreal and nightmarish works explore alienation, bureaucracy, and existential anxiety. The word 'Kafkaesque' derives from his vision.

Early Life

Kafka was born in Prague to a middle-class Jewish family. He studied law and worked in insurance while writing in his spare time, publishing little during his lifetime.

Impact

Kafka's works became defining texts of 20th-century literature. His exploration of absurdity, powerlessness, and alienation influenced existentialism, magical realism, and postmodern literature.
