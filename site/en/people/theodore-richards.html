Overview

Theodore William Richards was an American chemist who received the Nobel Prize for his accurate determinations of the atomic weights of many chemical elements.

Early Life

Born in Germantown, Pennsylvania. Studied at Harvard.

Impact

Richards's precise atomic weight measurements were essential for chemistry, confirming atomic theory and enabling the periodic table.
