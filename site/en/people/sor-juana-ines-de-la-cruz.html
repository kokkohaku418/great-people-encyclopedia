Sor Juana Ines de la Cruz

  • Lifespan: 1648 – 1695
  • Fields: Literature
  • Countries: Mexico

Overview

Sor Juana Ines de la Cruz was a Mexican nun, scholar, and poet considered the greatest literary figure of colonial Latin America.

Early Life

Born an illegitimate child in San Miguel Nepantla, she was a prodigy who learned to read at age three and entered a convent to pursue her intellectual life.

Impact

She championed women's right to education centuries before the feminist movement and produced poetry and philosophical works of enduring brilliance.
