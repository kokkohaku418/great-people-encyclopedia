Aristotle

  • Lifespan: -384 – -322
  • Fields: Philosophy, Biology
  • Countries: Greece

Overview

Aristotle was an ancient Greek philosopher and polymath whose writings cover physics, biology, metaphysics, logic, ethics, and politics. He was Plato's student and Alexander the Great's tutor.

Early Life

Aristotle was born in Stagira, Chalcidice. He joined Plato's Academy at age 17 and remained there for 20 years before founding his own school, the Lyceum.

Impact

Aristotle's systematic approach to knowledge created the foundation for Western science and philosophy. His logic dominated for two millennia, and his works on ethics and politics remain studied today.
