Ludwig Wittgenstein

  • Lifespan: 1889 – 1951
  • Fields: Philosophy
  • Countries: Austria, United Kingdom

Overview

Ludwig Wittgenstein was an Austrian-British philosopher who made revolutionary contributions to logic, philosophy of language, and philosophy of mind. He is considered one of the greatest philosophers of the 20th century.

Early Life

Wittgenstein was born in Vienna into one of Europe's wealthiest families. He studied engineering before turning to philosophy under Bertrand Russell at Cambridge.

Impact

Wittgenstein's two major works represent two distinct philosophical approaches: the logical atomism of the Tractatus and the language-game theory of the Investigations. Both transformed analytic philosophy.
