William James

  • Lifespan: 1842 – 1910
  • Fields: Philosophy
  • Countries: United States

Overview

William James was an American philosopher and psychologist who was a leading figure in pragmatism and the father of American psychology.

Early Life

James was born in New York City to a wealthy and intellectual family. He studied medicine at Harvard and later became professor of philosophy and psychology there.

Impact

James's pragmatism redefined truth in terms of practical consequences. His Principles of Psychology established psychology as a modern science and his philosophy influenced Dewey, Russell, and Wittgenstein.
