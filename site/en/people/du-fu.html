Du Fu

  • Lifespan: 712 – 770
  • Fields: Literature
  • Countries: China

Overview

Du Fu was a Chinese poet of the Tang dynasty, known as the Poet-Sage. His socially conscious poetry is considered among the greatest in the Chinese literary tradition.

Early Life

Du Fu was born into a scholar-official family. He spent years attempting to enter government service and later suffered greatly during the An Lushan Rebellion.

Impact

Du Fu's poetry documented the suffering of ordinary people during war and famine with unprecedented compassion and realism. He is revered as China's greatest realist poet.
