Marcel Proust

  • Lifespan: 1871 – 1922
  • Fields: Literature
  • Countries: France

Overview

Marcel Proust was a French novelist whose In Search of Lost Time is one of the most celebrated works of fiction in any language, exploring memory, time, and society.

Early Life

Proust was born in Paris to a wealthy family. Sickly from childhood, he became increasingly reclusive, spending his final years writing in a cork-lined bedroom.

Impact

In Search of Lost Time revolutionized the novel through its exploration of involuntary memory, subjective experience, and the passage of time. Its influence on modernist and postmodernist literature is immeasurable.
