John Ericsson

  • Lifespan: 1803 – 1889
  • Fields: Engineering
  • Countries: Sweden, United States

Overview

John Ericsson was a Swedish-American inventor and mechanical engineer who designed the ironclad warship USS Monitor, revolutionizing naval warfare.

Early Life

Born in Langbanshyttan, Sweden, he showed early engineering talent and moved to the United States where he focused on marine propulsion and warship design.

Impact

His Monitor design and its battle with the CSS Virginia marked the end of wooden warships and the beginning of the modern iron and steel navy.
