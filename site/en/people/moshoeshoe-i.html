Moshoeshoe I

  • Lifespan: 1786 – 1870
  • Fields: Politics
  • Countries: ls

Overview

Moshoeshoe I was the founder and first king of Basotho nation (modern Lesotho), renowned for his diplomatic skill in preserving his people's independence.

Early Life

Born Lepoqo in the Drakensberg Mountains, he gathered displaced peoples during the upheavals of the Mfecane into a unified nation.

Impact

He skillfully used diplomacy to protect his people from both Zulu expansion and Boer encroachment, establishing Lesotho as an enduring nation.
