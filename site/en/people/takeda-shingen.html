Takeda Shingen

  • Lifespan: 1521 – 1573
  • Fields: Politics
  • Countries: Japan

Overview

Takeda Shingen was a powerful daimyo of Kai Province, famous for his innovative cavalry tactics and his rivalry with Uesugi Kenshin.

Early Life

Born Takeda Harunobu, he overthrew his father to become lord of Kai and built one of the most formidable military forces of the Sengoku period.

Impact

His military innovations, especially in cavalry tactics and the use of the Furinkazan banner, influenced Japanese warfare profoundly.
