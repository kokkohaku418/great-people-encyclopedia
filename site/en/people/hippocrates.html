Hippocrates

  • Lifespan: -460 – -370
  • Fields: Biology
  • Countries: Greece

Overview

Hippocrates was an ancient Greek physician known as the 'Father of Medicine' who established medicine as a discipline separate from philosophy and superstition.

Early Life

Born on the island of Kos, he came from a family of physicians and studied under his father before traveling widely in Greece.

Impact

He established the ethical foundations of medical practice through the Hippocratic Oath and pioneered clinical observation and systematic diagnosis.
