Simone Weil

  • Lifespan: 1909 – 1943
  • Fields: Philosophy
  • Countries: France

Overview

Simone Weil was a French philosopher, mystic, and political activist whose writings on attention, affliction, and grace are among the most profound of the 20th century.

Early Life

Weil was born in Paris to a secular Jewish family. She studied at the École Normale Supérieure and taught philosophy while engaging in labor activism and the Spanish Civil War.

Impact

Weil's philosophical writings on attention, the relationship between affliction and grace, and the need for roots in community have influenced theology, ethics, and political thought. She died at 34.
