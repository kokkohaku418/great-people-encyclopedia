Plotinus

  • Lifespan: 204 – 270
  • Fields: Philosophy
  • Countries: Egypt, Italy

Overview

Plotinus was the founder of Neoplatonism, one of the most influential philosophical systems in Western and Islamic thought.

Early Life

Born in Lycopolis, Egypt. Studied in Alexandria under Ammonius Saccas for eleven years.

Impact

His philosophy of the One, emanation, and the soul's return deeply influenced Christian, Islamic, and Jewish theology.
