Jorge Luis Borges

  • Lifespan: 1899 – 1986
  • Fields: Literature
  • Countries: Argentina

Overview

Jorge Luis Borges was an Argentine short-story writer, poet, and essayist whose labyrinthine fictions exploring infinity, mirrors, and identity are among the most original in world literature.

Early Life

Borges was born in Buenos Aires. He spent his youth in Switzerland and Spain before returning to Argentina, where he worked as a librarian and began publishing stories.

Impact

Borges's short stories anticipated postmodernism and hypertext. His influence on world literature is immense, inspiring writers from Umberto Eco to Salman Rushdie.
