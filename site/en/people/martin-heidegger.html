Martin Heidegger

  • Lifespan: 1889 – 1976
  • Fields: Philosophy
  • Countries: Germany

Overview

Martin Heidegger was a German philosopher whose exploration of the question of Being is one of the most significant contributions to 20th-century philosophy.

Early Life

Heidegger was born in Meßkirch, Baden. He studied theology and philosophy at the University of Freiburg and became Edmund Husserl's assistant.

Impact

Heidegger's Being and Time fundamentally reoriented philosophy toward the question of existence. His influence extends through existentialism, hermeneutics, postmodernism, and environmental philosophy.
