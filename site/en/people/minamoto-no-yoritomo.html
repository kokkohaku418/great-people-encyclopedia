Minamoto no Yoritomo

  • Lifespan: 1147 – 1199
  • Fields: Politics
  • Countries: Japan

Overview

Minamoto no Yoritomo founded the Kamakura Shogunate, establishing the first military government in Japanese history.

Early Life

Born into the prestigious Minamoto clan, he was exiled as a teenager after his father's defeat in the Heiji Rebellion.

Impact

He created the shogunate system of military government that would define Japanese politics for nearly seven centuries.

Timeline
