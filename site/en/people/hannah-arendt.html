Hannah Arendt

  • Lifespan: 1906 – 1975
  • Fields: Philosophy, Politics
  • Countries: Germany, United States

Overview

Hannah Arendt was a German-American political theorist whose works on totalitarianism, the nature of power, and the banality of evil are among the most important in 20th-century political thought.

Early Life

Arendt was born in Hanover, Germany, to a secular Jewish family. She studied under Heidegger and Jaspers before fleeing Nazi Germany in 1933.

Impact

Arendt's analysis of totalitarianism, her concept of the banality of evil, and her theory of political action transformed political philosophy and our understanding of modern political phenomena.
