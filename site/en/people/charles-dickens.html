Charles Dickens

  • Lifespan: 1812 – 1870
  • Fields: Literature
  • Countries: United Kingdom

Overview

Charles Dickens was an English novelist and social critic who created some of the world's best-known fictional characters. He is regarded as the greatest novelist of the Victorian era.

Early Life

Dickens was born in Portsmouth. His father's imprisonment for debt forced young Charles to work in a factory, an experience that profoundly shaped his social conscience and writing.

Impact

Dickens's serialized novels reached massive audiences and raised awareness of social injustice. His characters and stories have become part of Western cultural consciousness.
