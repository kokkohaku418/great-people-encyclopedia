  • Lifespan: 1805 – 1875
  • Fields: Literature

Overview

Hans Christian Andersen was a Danish author known for his fairy tales.

Early Life

Born in Odense, Denmark in poverty.

Impact

His fairy tales are among the most translated works in literary history.
