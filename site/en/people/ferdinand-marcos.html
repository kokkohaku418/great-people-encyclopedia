Ferdinand Marcos

  • Lifespan: 1917 – 1989
  • Fields: Politics
  • Countries: Philippines

Overview

Ferdinand Marcos was the president of the Philippines who imposed martial law and was overthrown by a people's revolution.

Early Life

Born in Sarrat, Ilocos Norte. Studied law at the University of the Philippines.

Impact

His authoritarian rule and subsequent overthrow through the EDSA People Power Revolution became a model for nonviolent democratic change.
