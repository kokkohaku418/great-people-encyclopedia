Wu Zetian

  • Lifespan: 624 – 705
  • Fields: Politics
  • Countries: China

Overview

Wu Zetian was the only woman in Chinese history to assume the title of Empress Regnant, ruling China during the Tang Dynasty and founding the brief Zhou Dynasty.

Early Life

Born into a wealthy and noble family in Wenshui, Shanxi, she entered the imperial court as a concubine of Emperor Taizong at age fourteen.

Impact

She expanded the Chinese empire, reformed the civil service examination system, and promoted Buddhism, governing effectively for over four decades.
