Rabindranath Tagore

  • Lifespan: 1861 – 1941
  • Fields: Literature
  • Countries: India

Overview

Rabindranath Tagore was an Indian poet, writer, and philosopher who became the first non-European to win the Nobel Prize in Literature. He is the author of the national anthems of India and Bangladesh.

Early Life

Tagore was born in Calcutta to a prominent Bengali family. He began writing poetry at age eight and published his first collection at sixteen.

Impact

Tagore modernized Bengali literature and introduced Indian culture to the West. His poetry, music, and educational philosophy influenced Indian independence and global literary culture.
