Kangxi Emperor

  • Lifespan: 1654 – 1722
  • Fields: Politics
  • Countries: China

Overview

The Kangxi Emperor was the longest-reigning emperor of China and one of the most capable rulers of the Qing Dynasty.

Early Life

Born Xuanye, he ascended to the throne at age seven after his father's death from smallpox and took personal control of the government at fifteen.

Impact

He consolidated Qing rule over China, expanded the empire's borders, patronized arts and sciences, and compiled the monumental Kangxi Dictionary.
