Nagarjuna

  • Lifespan: 150 – 250
  • Fields: Philosophy
  • Countries: India

Overview

Nagarjuna was an Indian Buddhist philosopher who founded the Madhyamaka school of Mahayana Buddhism. He is widely regarded as the most important Buddhist philosopher after the Buddha himself.

Early Life

Little is known about Nagarjuna's early life. He is traditionally believed to have been born in South India and to have become a Buddhist monk and scholar at Nalanda.

Impact

Nagarjuna's concept of sunyata (emptiness) and his dialectical method profoundly shaped Mahayana Buddhism across East and Central Asia. His philosophy influenced all subsequent Buddhist thought.
