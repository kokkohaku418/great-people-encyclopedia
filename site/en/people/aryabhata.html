Aryabhata

  • Lifespan: 476 – 550
  • Fields: Mathematics, Astronomy
  • Countries: India

Overview

Aryabhata was an Indian mathematician and astronomer who made groundbreaking contributions to both fields.

Early Life

Born in Kusumapura (modern Patna). Studied at the ancient university of Nalanda.

Impact

He introduced the concept of zero as a placeholder, calculated pi to remarkable accuracy, and proposed that the Earth rotates on its axis.
