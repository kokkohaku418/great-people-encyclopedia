Guru Nanak

  • Lifespan: 1469 – 1539
  • Fields: Philosophy
  • Countries: India

Overview

Guru Nanak was the founder of Sikhism and the first of the ten Sikh Gurus.

Early Life

Born in Talwandi (now Nankana Sahib, Pakistan). Showed spiritual inclinations from childhood.

Impact

He founded one of the world's major religions, emphasizing equality, devotion, and honest living.
